## Company Overview

Woozza builds unmanned, real-time monitoring systems that help prevent slope collapse, landslides, and flooding. The systems measure atmospheric and ground conditions on site and automate the measurement and reporting steps that field teams used to do by hand([KICT Smart Construction Support Center](https://www.kict.re.kr/gallery.es?act=view&b_list=6&bid=tnnt&keyField=&keyWord=&list_no=14421&mid=a10407040200&nPage=1&orderby=&tag=)). The company is a certified weather-instrument maker recognized by the Korea Meteorological Administration([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202312071818003)).

## Technology & Product

- **Unmanned atmospheric and ground monitoring system**: Real-time sensing of weather and geotechnical conditions for slope-collapse disaster prevention, with automated measurement and reporting([KICT Smart Construction Support Center](https://www.kict.re.kr/gallery.es?act=view&b_list=6&bid=tnnt&keyField=&keyWord=&list_no=14421&mid=a10407040200&nPage=1&orderby=&tag=)).
- **Unmanned inclinometer**: Detects ground movement in real time across sites such as retaining walls and urban excavation zones, applying automation to installation, measurement, and reporting([SEN TV](https://www.sentv.co.kr/article/view/sentv202504160105)).
- **Smart rain gauge and weather instruments**: Equipment for rainfall observation and atmospheric data collection([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202312071818003)).
- **3D atmospheric data visualization**: Volume-rendering technology to visualize atmospheric environment data, developed through a technology transfer with Inje University([Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/policy/2023/01/31/2023013115292163976)).

## Market & Use Cases

The systems target construction sites, civil-engineering projects, and public infrastructure exposed to slope and weather risk. Typical use cases include monitoring excavation and retaining walls for ground movement, observing rainfall to predict slope failure, and replacing manual field inspection with automated, continuous measurement([SEN TV](https://www.sentv.co.kr/article/view/sentv202504160105)). The visualization technology can support typhoon, rainfall, snowfall, and fine-dust measurement([Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/policy/2023/01/31/2023013115292163976)).

## Traction & References

- Woozza won the top award (KICT President's Award) at the 2023 Smart Construction Startup Idea Contest, hosted by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202312071818003)).
- It is an off-site startup tenant at KICT's Smart Construction Support Center, with access to mentoring, researcher matching, and prototyping support([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202312071818003)).
- In January 2023, the company signed a technology-transfer agreement with Inje University's industry cooperation foundation for 3D atmospheric data visualization([Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/policy/2023/01/31/2023013115292163976)).
