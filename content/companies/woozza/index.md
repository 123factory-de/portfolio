## Company Overview

Woozza operates an urban disaster-response platform built on high-density rainfall observation and AI flood prediction. It runs `FLOOD WATCH` and provides minute-level rainfall data and real-time flood-risk information. It supports early action by public officials and connects sensors, data collection, prediction models, alerts, and evacuation support in one system.

## Technology & Product

Its core hardware is a WMO-standard smart rain gauge. Woozza deploys the gauges at much higher density than conventional public weather networks and collects rainfall data every minute. The devices support LTE, 5G, and LoRa wireless communication, with solar-powered field operation.

On top of the sensing layer, Woozza runs an AI-based flood prediction system. It analyzes rainfall data, road water levels, and local risk information to issue early flood forecasts. It also connects care officials, community partners, and vulnerable residents through an evacuation-support workflow.

## Market & Use Cases

Its main users are local governments, disaster-response agencies, and public infrastructure operators. The platform supports district-level urban flood management, evacuation support for vulnerable populations, and real-time decision support for public-safety teams.

Woozza also serves power plants, rail facilities, and industrial complexes with high flood risk. The same framework extends into smart-city weather infrastructure, water management, and climate-risk services.

- Its operating model includes a disaster-response workflow for vulnerable households in Seoul, including flood forecast thresholds, alert routing, and evacuation support coordination for care officials and community partners.
