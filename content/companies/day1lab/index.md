## Company Overview

DAY1LAB develops biodegradable plastic alternatives based on starch and other plant-derived natural polymers. Its materials aim to reduce plastic waste, microplastics, and carbon emissions while still working with practical production needs.

The company focuses on a plastic-free material shift. It targets packaging and consumer products that need alternatives to petroleum-based plastic. Its materials are designed to break down in soil, marine, composting, and other controlled environments.

## Technology & Product

DAY1LAB's core material is RETARCH, a thermoplastic starch-based natural polymer that degrades without generating microplastics. The company works from base materials to production-ready compounds and finished products.

Key product and technology areas include:

- **RETARCH resin grade**: a 100% biobased starch-based composite material used as base resin or additive.
- **RETARCH compound grade**: production-grade pellets optimized for manufacturing processes.
- **High-barrier coating and film materials**: film materials that block oxygen and moisture for food packaging.
- **Final products**: finished goods produced on conventional manufacturing lines, including blown film extrusion, T-die extrusion, and injection molding.

## Market & Use Cases

DAY1LAB is relevant to food packaging companies, consumer goods brands, converters, retailers, and manufacturers. These customers need plastic alternatives that can work with existing production lines.

The company focuses on low-carbon materials, microplastic-free degradation, and compatibility with composting, anaerobic digestion, soil, and marine environments. Near-term applications include packaging, food containers, films, and brand-led plastic reduction programs.
