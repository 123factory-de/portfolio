## Company Overview

DAY1LAB is an alternative plastic materials company focused on natural polymer technologies. The company develops materials that reduce plastic-related carbon emissions and waste while avoiding microplastics, heavy metals, and hazardous substances.

Its core direction is to create materials that originate from nature and can return safely to nature across multiple degradation environments.

## Technology & Product

DAY1LAB's primary material platform is RETARCH, a natural polymer material based on starch-cellulose composite technology. The company works across the value chain from base materials to production-ready compounds and finished products.

The business areas include:

- **Resin grade RETARCH**: a 100% biobased starch-cellulose composite material used as base resin or additive.
- **Compound grade RETARCH**: production-grade pellets optimized for manufacturing processes.
- **Final products**: finished goods produced on conventional petroleum-based manufacturing lines, including blown film extrusion, T-die extrusion, and injection molding.

The company emphasizes carbon reduction, microplastic elimination, and degradation under industrial composting, home composting, anaerobic digestion, and other environments.

## Strategic Highlights

- Certifications include USDA Biobased, TÜV AUSTRIA OK COMPOST HOME/INDUSTRIAL, BPI commercial compostability, Korean environmental labels, ISO 9001, and ISO 14001.
- DAY1LAB won a CES 2024 Innovation Award in Sustainability, Eco-Design & Smart Energy.
- The company secured Series A investment and has built domestic and international IP around its materials.
- Reported tests and certifications include non-detection of microplastics and hazardous substances, green technology certification, and anaerobic digestion analysis.
- Main product: plastic alternatives using natural polymer carbohydrates.
