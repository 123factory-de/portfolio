## Company Overview

Perseus develops software solutions for **Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs)**, focusing on virtualization, functional safety, and the consolidation of vehicle computing architectures.

## Technology & Product

Its core technology is a **multi-domain hypervisor** that enables a high-capacity QM-grade operating system, such as Linux or Android, and a safety-grade Safety RTOS to run on a single SoC through secure software partitioning. By replacing multiple dedicated ECUs or boards with a consolidated computing platform, the solution can reduce semiconductor costs, wiring complexity, vehicle weight, and the effort required for functional-safety certification.

Perseus' hypervisor supports both CPU and MCU architectures and is designed for safety-critical automotive applications.

## Market & Use Cases

The technology is designed for next-generation **SDV architectures, digital cockpits, domain controllers, and centralized vehicle computing platforms**. It can support the consolidation of infotainment and safety-critical functions within a common hardware architecture.

## Traction & References

Perseus' hypervisor has achieved **ASIL-D safety certification** and is already commercially shipping. The company has technology partnerships with **Infineon, NXP, Renesas, and Arm**, and has worked with automotive companies including **Hyundai, Mando, and LG**. Its technology maturity is assessed at approximately **TRL 6–7**.

## Collaboration Relevance

For Mercedes-Benz, potential applications include using Perseus' hypervisor to consolidate **safety-critical software and infotainment functions within MB.OS-based vehicle computing architectures**. This could support the development of domain controllers with reduced hardware complexity, cost, and weight.
