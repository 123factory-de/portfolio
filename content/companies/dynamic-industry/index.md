## Company Overview

Dynamic Industry builds physical, eco-friendly recycling systems for decommissioned solar panels. Its equipment dismantles, crushes, and separates panels to recover high-purity glass, aluminum, silicon, silver, and copper. The process uses no toxic chemicals and no high-temperature incineration.

## Technology & Product

Dynamic Industry offers physical solar panel recycling systems:

- **Dismantling & Glass Separation**: Machinery automates the removal of aluminum frames and junction boxes, followed by glass separation.
- **Mechanical Sorting**: Separation lines crush and sort panel materials using physical forces, such as vibration and gravity density sorting, to isolate valuable metals.
- **Material Recovery**: The system recovers key materials such as silicon, glass, aluminum, silver, and copper. Reclaimed metals are refined to industrial grades for reuse in manufacturing.

## Market & Use Cases

The company works with solar park operators, utility providers, recycling facilities, and waste management companies. By using physical sorting instead of chemical leaching, the system lowers carbon emissions and helps operators meet electronic waste regulations.
