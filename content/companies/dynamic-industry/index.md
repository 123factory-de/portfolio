## Company Overview

Dynamic Industry develops physical, eco-friendly recycling systems for decommissioned solar panels. The company provides mechanized equipment and process solutions that dismantle, crush, and separate solar panels to recover high-purity glass, aluminum, silicon, silver, and copper without using toxic chemicals or high-temperature incineration.

## Technology & Product

Dynamic Industry offers physical solar panel recycling systems:

- **Dismantling & Glass Separation**: Machinery automates the removal of aluminum frames and junction boxes, followed by glass separation ([Dynamic Industry](https://dynamicindustry.kr/)).
- **Mechanical Sorting**: Mechanized separation lines crush and sort panel materials using physical forces (such as vibration and gravity density sorting) to isolate valuable metals ([Dynamic Industry](https://dynamicindustry.kr/)).
- **Material Recovery**: The system recovers six primary materials: silicon, glass, aluminum, silver, and copper. Reclaimed metals are refined to industrial grades for reuse in manufacturing ([Dynamic Industry](https://dynamicindustry.kr/)).

## Market & Use Cases

The company works with solar park operators, utility providers, recycling facilities, and waste management companies. By utilizing physical sorting instead of chemical leaching, Dynamic Industry's system lowers carbon emissions and helps operators comply with electronic waste regulations.
