## Company Overview

E material LAB is a deep-tech startup that develops passive cooling components to replace conventional aluminum heat sinks. Its core idea is to coat metal with a nano-porous layer that radiates heat, so parts can be lighter and thinner while cooling better. It targets electronics that generate a lot of heat, such as semiconductors, data centers, and electric vehicles.

## Technology & Product

The core material is BLACK metal, a nano-porous siloxane-resin coating on aluminum that raises heat emissivity. Built on this material, the HEAT suppressor is a passive cooling component designed to replace bulky metal heat sinks. The company's Double-Radiation-Cooling approach, together with a surface pattern it calls Fanless-FAN, moves air by radiation with no moving parts. The company reports emissivity up to 0.98, about half the weight of a metal heat sink, and higher cooling performance. The HEAT suppressor comes in three forms: a structure type for semiconductors, a sheet type for curved or flexible use, and an add-on part for modules and housings.

## Market & Use Cases

The technology targets products where heat limits performance: semiconductors and IC packaging, data centers and AI computing, automotive and mobility, consumer electronics, LED and display, and robotics. It is sold both as a raw coated material for OEM use and as finished components. Because it is passive and needs no fan, it fits sealed or space-constrained designs where fans are hard to add.

## Traction & References

The company reports six registered Korean patents, with four more applied and two licensed. It received a Minister of SMEs and Startups Award for technology innovation in 2023. It has secured government R&D grants from the Ministry of Science and ICT and completed technology transfers from KBSI and KRICT. In 2024 it was selected for the TIPS R&D program and secured investment. In 2025 it began joint development with Fraunhofer and the AIT Austrian Institute of Technology. Its thermal test results have been checked by Korean testing institutes including KATECH, KOPTI, and KTRI.

## Collaboration Relevance

E material LAB fits electronics, semiconductor, and automotive manufacturers that need to remove heat without adding fans, weight, or bulk. It offers the material for OEM use, finished components, and IP or technology licensing, along with joint R&D. It is most relevant to thermally constrained products such as data center and AI hardware, EV electronics, and compact devices.
