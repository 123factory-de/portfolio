## Company Overview

WISHBUILDER works on water, sanitation, circular resources, and carbon reduction. Its projects include water purification products and low-carbon biomass fuel made from waste resources.

The company connects social impact with climate impact. It works on clean water access, waste upcycling, and alternatives to wood fuel.

## Technology & Product

Core product lines include:

- **PURE WISH**: a water purification product for contaminated water.
- **WISH WELL**: a mobile and modular water purification system.
- **WISH BURN**: a low-carbon solid biomass fuel using waste coffee grounds and shell waste.

WISH BURN targets a circular fuel model. It can reduce wood fuel use, waste disposal, and carbon emissions.

## Market & Use Cases

WISHBUILDER is relevant to water, sanitation, outdoor fuel, waste upcycling, and social impact programs. Early markets include Korea's camping and outdoor fuel market, with longer-term use in coffee-producing countries in Africa and Southeast Asia.

Use cases include emergency water purification, community water support, biomass fuel, and circular-resource pilots.
