## Company Overview

DaWinKS is a South Korean fintech company that connects fiat currency, digital assets, identity verification, and retail payments. Its DPEC Platform links MTM/DTM terminals, crypto POS and QR payment tools, and identity-document scanners. The company began with patent-based scanner supply in 2005 and later expanded into blockchain financial infrastructure.

## Technology & Product

The DPEC Platform supports currency exchange, remittance, payment, stored-value top-up, and digital-asset cash on/off-ramp workflows. Its MTM/DTM terminals can combine foreign-currency exchange and digital-asset conversion with services such as prepaid transportation-card issuance, tax refunds, and overseas remittance.

The identity layer combines a passport and ID combo scanner with AI facial matching and 3D liveness. Infrared and hologram inspection help detect altered documents. Wallet screening, sanctions-list filtering, and fraud detection support KYC and AML processes. DaWinKS also offers crypto POS and QR Pay tools for merchant payments.

## Market & Use Cases

DaWinKS targets banks, exchange and remittance operators, retailers, airports, hotels, duty-free stores, and tourism businesses. A terminal can serve international visitors who need to exchange cash, withdraw digital assets, receive a tax refund, top up a transportation card, or send money after identity verification.

The same platform can support merchant payments and regulated digital-asset access in physical locations. It is most relevant where a partner needs both a customer-facing terminal and a compliance layer.

## Traction & References

DaWinKS states that its identity and passport scanners are used at about 670 locations. Listed references include court registry offices, Korea Securities Depository, airport processes, Kangwon Land, tax-refund stores, and unattended lending kiosks.

The company lists MTM/DTM deployments or operating agreements with Emart24, Homeplus, Lotte Mart, Eland Retail, and CJ Foodville's N Seoul Tower. It also lists collaborations with Kona I for prepaid cards, Global Tax Free and PayGate for visitor services, Kaia and Payletter for digital-asset services, and Korea Digital Asset (KODA) for custody([DaWinKS press releases](https://www.dawinks.com/news_en.html)).

DaWinKS holds TTA certification for an identity-card authenticity terminal and is registered with the Korea Customs Service as an unattended currency-exchange operator. In 2024, it received a temporary ICT Regulatory Sandbox permit for AI facial-recognition-based remote real-name verification([ICT Regulatory Sandbox committee release](https://www.korcham.net/new_pdf/target/%2812%EC%9B%9421%EC%9D%BC%EC%9E%90%29ICT%20%EA%B7%9C%EC%A0%9C%EC%83%8C%EB%93%9C%EB%B0%95%EC%8A%A4%20%EC%8B%AC%EC%9D%98%EC%9C%84%EC%9B%90%ED%9A%8C.pdf)). The company was also selected for the 2026 Gangnam-gu Global Testbed program.

## Collaboration Relevance

Financial institutions and licensed exchange or remittance operators can test co-branded MTM/DTM services. Retail and tourism networks can pilot foreign-currency exchange, digital-asset withdrawal, tax refund, or transportation-card services at selected locations.

Other collaboration options include connecting stablecoins or blockchain networks to the DPEC Platform, integrating custody and transaction-screening services, and licensing terminal or identity-verification technology. Overseas pilots will require a local partner with the relevant financial licenses, liquidity, and physical distribution network.
