## Company Overview

P2ACH AI turns physical spaces into measurable advertising media using vision AI and edge computing. The technology analyzes video on the device itself and converts it into anonymized metadata, so raw footage never leaves the sensor([P2ACH AI](https://p2ach.ai)).

This on-device approach removes the privacy risk of sending camera video to a central server. It lets advertisers and retailers measure real attention and engagement for out-of-home advertising and in-store media.

## Technology & Product

The [P2ACH AI platform](https://p2ach.ai) runs on an edge AI box and combines several modules. Audience analytics estimate visitor age and gender in an anonymized way. Re-ID technology tracks movement across multiple cameras to count unique visitors without duplicates. Real-time targeting delivers content based on who is actually in front of the screen([P2ACH AI](https://p2ach.ai)).

For retail, the platform adds smart shelf and stock analysis, spatial heatmaps of foot traffic, and an AI agent that turns the data into optimization suggestions. For digital out-of-home (DOOH) media, the system measures how many people look at an ad, for how long, and with what attention, so advertising performance can be quantified.

## Market & Use Cases

The main customers are DOOH media owners, retailers, and brands that need proof of advertising performance offline. Typical use cases include measuring ad attention on outdoor screens, analyzing shopper behavior inside stores, preventing out-of-stock situations, and improving merchandising layouts.

Useful pilot settings include retail chains that want shopper-journey data and transit or outdoor media networks that need audience measurement for their screens.

## Traction & References

P2ACH AI lists partners and customers including AWS, GS Retail, Hanwha Vision, LOTTE, Shinhan, and Seoul Metro([P2ACH AI](https://p2ach.ai)). Reported deployment results include higher viewer interest, higher sales for advertised products, and lower operating cost([P2ACH AI](https://p2ach.ai)).

The company has run overseas pilots and installation contracts in Japan, including a railway line in Shizuoka and a large convenience-store chain([StartupN](https://www.startupn.kr)). It was selected for the Ministry of SMEs and Startups Deep-tech TIPS program([RndCircle](https://www.rndcircle.io)) and closed a Pre-Series A round of about KRW 3 billion in 2025([Platum](https://platum.kr)). It was also selected for the 2026 Gangnam-gu Test-Bed program.
