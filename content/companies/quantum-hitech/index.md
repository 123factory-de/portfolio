## Company Overview

Quantum Hi-Tech develops data-driven solutions for **EV battery health assessment**, helping vehicle owners, fleets, and automotive service providers understand battery condition and degradation using real-world driving data.

## Technology & Product

Its **TRIZ-AI** platform analyzes real-driving BMS data to assess **battery State of Health (SOH), degradation patterns, driving characteristics, and abnormal behavior** without removing or disassembling the battery.

The platform provides a **Battery Health Dashboard** and vehicle-specific **AI Battery Health Reports**, translating battery data into information that can be used for vehicle management, maintenance, and customer-facing services.

## Market & Use Cases

Key applications include **certified pre-owned EVs, fleet management, after-sales services, vehicle maintenance, battery certification, and EV resale**. The technology can improve transparency around battery condition and support more informed decisions across the EV lifecycle.

## Traction & References

Quantum Hi-Tech has built a real-world dataset covering **more than 1,000 EVs** and operates its cloud-based battery analytics service through live pilots. The company received a **CES 2026 Innovation Award** and is exploring additional applications including insurance, charging services, and battery passports. Technology maturity is assessed at approximately **TRL 7–8**.

## Collaboration Relevance

For Mercedes-Benz, Quantum Hi-Tech could support **EV battery health reporting for customers and certified used vehicles**, as well as fleet and after-sales services. The solution could provide battery health information based on everyday driving data and help strengthen transparency around EV battery condition and resale value.
