## Company Overview

ieumBio helps researchers who study aging and how to slow it down. It runs the lab experiments they need before any human trials can begin (this early stage is called "preclinical"). The company started at UNIST in 2024 and is based on the university campus in Ulsan.

Its work centers on the African turquoise killifish, a small fish that lives only about four to six months. Because it is a vertebrate (an animal with a backbone, like us), results from the fish are more relevant to humans than results from simpler creatures. And because it dies so young, researchers can see the full effect on its lifespan in a matter of months. ieumBio does these experiments on behalf of other companies and labs, a setup known in the industry as a CRO (contract research organization).

## Technology & Product

The main product is [KilliSpan](https://ieumbio.com/services/killispan/). It is an automated service that feeds a compound to killifish and checks whether the fish live longer. The process is automated so many compounds can be tested at once (what scientists call "high-throughput"). Because the fish is a backboned animal that already has a very short life, the test shows real effects on a whole living animal far faster than the usual mouse experiments.

Customers can pick from two levels of service: a quick first screen to spot promising compounds, and a larger, more rigorous test to confirm the results. The compound can be given by mouth or by injection. About 16 weeks after a study begins, ieumBio sends back an early report on how the fish's lifespan changed. It also offers add-on tests of the fish's behavior and genes, and it keeps tissue samples so researchers can later look at how a compound works in the body and examine the tissues under a microscope.

## Market & Use Cases

ieumBio's customers are universities and companies working on aging. Their projects include screening drugs meant to slow aging, testing whether natural ingredients actually work, and studying what causes aging in the first place. On the industry side, ieumBio serves makers of drugs, health supplements, and cosmetics. Its main appeal is simple: it delivers lifespan results from a whole, living animal faster than older methods can.

The company holds registered trademarks for "ieumBio" and "KilliSpan," with international trademark filings and several patents and design registrations in progress([ieumBio](https://ieumbio.com/0102?l=e)). It has signed more than a dozen research collaboration agreements, including Seoul National University, Konkuk University Hospital, Inha University, and Spain's National Cancer Research Centre (CNIO)([UNIST](http://www.unist.ac.kr/unist/center/community.do?mode=view&articleNo=246281)).

## Collaboration Relevance

ieumBio is a good fit for joint research with universities and companies, and for testing work done on their behalf. Its killifish service is already a finished product, with a rigorous confirmation step and follow-up support to study how a compound works and to examine tissues. For companies weighing new longevity compounds, ieumBio does not sell or license the compounds; it tests them in living animals in a standardized way. That makes it a practical partner for both quick screening and careful confirmation.
