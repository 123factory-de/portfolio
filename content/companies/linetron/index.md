## Company Overview

Linetron develops software solutions for **automotive software testing and validation**, with a focus on automating the increasingly complex verification requirements of Software-Defined Vehicles.

## Technology & Product

Its core solution, **AutoTest**, is an AI-based test automation platform for vehicle and functional-safety ECU software. The system automatically generates test scenarios based on ECU function specifications, operating conditions, and simulation data, then executes and analyzes repeated verification tests.

By automating regression testing, AutoTest addresses the growing testing workload associated with frequent software updates and OTA deployments.

## Market & Use Cases

The technology can support **SDV software validation, ECU testing, functional-safety verification, regression testing, and OTA software development**. It is designed to work with established automotive testing environments and tools, including **dSPACE, Vector, and ETAS**.

## Traction & References

Linetron brings more than **20 years of automotive software experience**. Its technology can reduce test preparation time from months to weeks and automate repetitive testing across the software validation cycle. The core solution is currently at approximately **TRL 5**, with further development toward TRL 7 for real-world deployment.

## Collaboration Relevance

For Mercedes-Benz, Linetron's AutoTest could support **faster validation of MB.OS and vehicle software updates** by automatically generating, executing, and analyzing test scenarios. This could help reduce manual testing workload as software complexity and OTA update frequency increase.
