## Company Overview

Chingoohaja builds services for older adults, families, and care providers. The company combines social connection with AI-based monitoring.

Its work is relevant to aging societies where many seniors live alone or have limited daily contact. The service aims to support emotional well-being, early risk detection, and family communication.

## Technology & Product

[Chingoohaja](https://www.chingoohaja.com/) operates "Mohani," an interest-based conversation and matching service. The company is also developing AI phone monitoring that analyzes speech, emotion, and conversation patterns.

The system can help detect possible changes in cognitive or emotional state. This gives families and care organizations an additional signal without requiring constant in-person monitoring.

## Market & Use Cases

The main users are seniors, families, care organizations, hospitals, telecom companies, and insurers. Use cases include regular check-ins, emotional-care programs, early warning signals, and community-based senior support.

Climate resilience is also relevant. During heat waves or other climate-related risks, remote check-ins can help identify seniors who may need support.

## Traction & References

Chingoohaja is in private beta and demonstrator stage. The team has completed more than 250 interviews and MVP tests.

The company has received startup support funding, office support, and the National Assembly Speaker Award. Samsung Medical Center, Ulsan CCEI, Seoul AI Hub, DDM, and senior-care organizations are part of its collaboration network.

## Collaboration Relevance

Chingoohaja can work with healthcare providers, senior-care organizations, telecom operators, insurers, and local governments. A useful PoC would test phone-based monitoring with a defined senior group and compare alerts with caregiver feedback.

For Korea-Europe collaboration, the company may fit aging-care innovation, remote care, and climate-risk support for vulnerable groups.
