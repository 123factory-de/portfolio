## Company Overview

APGREEN develops on-site, distributed hydrogen production and power generation systems. The company integrates catalytic reforming and fuel cells to deliver clean, low-carbon energy solutions directly to customer facilities without requiring large-scale hydrogen delivery infrastructure.

## Technology & Product

APGREEN manufactures the **APG-Series**, a containerized hydrogen production and power system:

- **Catalytic Reforming**: The system utilizes Partial Oxidation (POX) technology to reform LNG or biogas into hydrogen with low energy requirements and high thermal efficiency ([APGREEN](https://apgreen.net/)).
- **Fuel-Cell Integration**: Produced hydrogen can be routed directly into integrated fuel cells to generate electricity on-site ([APGREEN](https://apgreen.net/)).
- **Modular Design**: The APG-Series features an all-in-one container design (including models APG-5, APG-20, APG-50, and APG-100) that is mobile and easy to install ([APGREEN](https://apgreen.net/)).

## Market & Use Cases

APGREEN targets distributed energy consumers, hydrogen refueling stations, industrial plants, fuel-cell power generators, and biogas processing facilities. The modular containers allow operators to generate electricity and hydrogen locally, bypassing high transportation and logistics costs.
