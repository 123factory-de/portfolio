## Company Overview

APGREEN develops on-site, distributed hydrogen production and power generation systems. The company combines catalytic reforming and fuel cells to supply clean, low-carbon energy at customer facilities, without the need for large-scale hydrogen delivery infrastructure.

## Technology & Product

APGREEN manufactures the **APG-Series**, a containerized hydrogen production and power system:

- **Catalytic Reforming**: The system uses Partial Oxidation (POX) to reform LNG or biogas into hydrogen with low energy use and high thermal efficiency.
- **Fuel-Cell Integration**: Hydrogen feeds directly into integrated fuel cells to generate electricity on-site.
- **Modular Design**: The APG-Series uses an all-in-one container design (models APG-5, APG-20, APG-50, and APG-100) that is mobile and easy to install.

## Market & Use Cases

APGREEN targets distributed energy consumers, hydrogen refueling stations, industrial plants, fuel-cell power generators, and biogas processing facilities. The modular containers let operators generate electricity and hydrogen locally, cutting transportation and logistics costs.
