## Company Overview

APGREEN is a distributed on-site hydrogen and power solution company that leverages existing gas infrastructure. The company combines Catalytic Partial Oxidation (CPOX) reforming and fuel cells to produce hydrogen and electricity directly at customer facilities from city gas, LNG, or biomethane — without the need for large-scale hydrogen delivery infrastructure.

## Technology & Product

APGREEN manufactures the **APG-Series**, a containerized hydrogen production and power system:

- **CPOX Reforming (Catalytic Partial Oxidation)**: The system reforms city gas, LNG, or biomethane into hydrogen with low energy use and high thermal efficiency.
- **Fuel-Cell Integration**: Hydrogen feeds directly into integrated fuel cells to generate electricity on-site.
- **Modular Design**: The APG-Series uses an all-in-one container design (models APG-5, APG-20, APG-100, and APG-300) that is mobile and easy to install.

Hydrogen produced from city gas or LNG is natural-gas-based. When biomethane is used as the feedstock, the same systems enable a low-carbon hydrogen pathway.

## Market & Use Cases

APGREEN targets distributed energy consumers, hydrogen refueling stations, industrial plants, fuel-cell power generators, and biogas processing facilities. The modular containers let operators generate electricity and hydrogen locally, cutting transportation and logistics costs.
