## Company Overview

POEN specializes in electric vehicle (EV) battery remanufacturing, recycling, and circular economy solutions. The company assesses the health of end-of-life or damaged EV battery packs, repair and rebuilds them into refurbished automotive batteries, or repurposes them for stationary Energy Storage Systems (ESS).

## Technology & Product

POEN provides a range of battery diagnostics and remanufacturing services:

- **Battery Remanufacturing**: Rebuilding used EV battery packs into fresh, quality-tested packs that meet automotive standards ([POEN](https://poen.co.kr/en/)).
- **Safety & Diagnostic Testing**: Evaluating residual capacity and health profiles to identify cells or modules suitable for reuse or repurposing ([POEN](https://poen.co.kr/en/)).
- **Second-Life Storage**: Converting decommissioned battery packs into energy storage solutions for residential, commercial, or micro-grid installations ([POEN](https://poen.co.kr/en/)).

## Market & Use Cases

POEN works closely with automotive OEMs, battery manufacturers, and fleet operators who face increasing pressure to manage battery lifecycles and comply with environmental regulations. By remanufacturing battery packs, POEN helps manufacturers and car owners avoid the high cost of brand-new replacements.
