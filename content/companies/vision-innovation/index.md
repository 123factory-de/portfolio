## Company Overview

Vision Innovation develops functional injection-molding nozzle solutions that enable manufacturers to increase the use of **recycled plastics in automotive components**. Its technology focuses on removing gases, moisture, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) released by recycled resins during the injection-molding process, helping improve product quality while reducing energy consumption and manufacturing costs.

## Technology & Product

Its core solution, **Vision Nozzle**, is a multi-type functional nozzle available in pin-type and screw-type configurations. Installed directly at the injection stage, the nozzle removes gases, moisture, and VOCs from recycled resin immediately before injection. Combined with a dedicated controller and AI-based process data analysis, the system aims to replace conventional pre-drying processes without requiring modifications to existing injection-molding machines.

By reducing drying time and improving process stability, Vision Nozzle enables manufacturers to increase recycled plastic content while maintaining consistent molding quality.

## Market & Use Cases

The technology can support **automotive interior and exterior plastic components, recycled plastic processing, injection-molding process optimization, VOC reduction, and sustainable manufacturing**. Potential applications include increasing post-consumer recycled (PCR) and post-industrial recycled (PIR) material content in automotive supplier production lines, while reducing defects, drying energy consumption, and material waste.

## Traction & References

Vision Innovation has validated its technology at **five sites in Korea and overseas**, reporting a reduction in defect rates from 4.7% to 1.1% and an increase in production output by approximately 1.8 times. Testing witnessed by KTR demonstrated **CO gas removal exceeding 30%**, reaching approximately 40% under specified PP+GF30 conditions. The company also reports that its technology can reduce pre-drying time from 2–8 hours to 0–2 hours and increase recycled material content from approximately 10% to over 30%.

Commercial traction includes an **export contract in Vietnam, purchase orders in China, and an MOU in the EU**. The core pin-type nozzle is at approximately **TRL 6–7**, with further development underway for larger-capacity nozzle configurations and AI-based process control.

## Collaboration Relevance

For Mercedes-Benz and its automotive supplier network, Vision Innovation's Vision Nozzle could enable **higher recycled-plastic content in injection-molded components** while reducing manufacturing defects, drying energy consumption, and material costs. A pilot on existing supplier production lines could help assess its contribution to recycled-material targets, VOC reduction, and the EU End-of-Life Vehicles (ELV) regulatory requirements without requiring major changes to existing equipment.
