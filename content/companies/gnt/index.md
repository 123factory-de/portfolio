## Company Overview

GnT develops and manufactures core mobility components designed to improve electric vehicle (EV) efficiency and support regenerative braking. The company focuses on automated manufacturing processes for high-reliability automotive electronics.

## Technology & Product

GnT offers three main product categories:

- **Converters**: The company develops Integrated DC-DC Converters (IDC) that combine On-Board Chargers (OBC) and Low-voltage DC-DC Converters (LDC) into a unified system ([GnT](https://thegntcorp.com/)).
- **Transformer Inductors**: These components convert alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC) for regenerative braking systems ([GnT](https://thegntcorp.com/)).
- **Circuit Boards**: Automated assembly lines produce custom printed circuit boards (PCBs) for automotive power electronics ([GnT](https://thegntcorp.com/)).

## Market & Use Cases

GnT serves automotive OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers in the EV supply chain. Its components are used in passenger and commercial electric vehicles to manage battery power conversion and capture kinetic energy during braking.
