## Company Overview

SIG Corporation is a Korean smart healthcare company focused on senior care. Its main product, SENIHUG, is a contactless sensor pad that monitors the sleep and vital signs of elderly people without any wearable device.

The company serves care facilities, hospitals, and local governments that need to watch over seniors, people living alone, and long-term care residents. The goal is to detect risk situations early and help caregivers respond faster.

## Technology & Product

SENIHUG is a sensor pad placed under the mattress. It measures biometric data without a wearable, a charger, or a camera. The pad connects over Wi-Fi and sends data to the server every three seconds, and it reconnects automatically if the connection drops.

The pad captures a single vibration signal that contains chest movement from breathing along with noise. A proprietary algorithm based on Variational Mode Decomposition separates heart rate and respiration from this signal. The AI improves measurement accuracy as it learns, and it derives heart rate variability to help flag signs of depression, anxiety, and stress.

Caregivers view all data in real time through a web dashboard and a mobile app. One screen can monitor many devices at once, and families can also follow a user through the app. When the system detects an anomaly, it sends an alert within about five seconds so staff can act quickly.

## Market & Use Cases

The core market is elderly care. Typical customers are nursing homes and care facilities, hospitals, and public agencies that run welfare and safety programs for seniors.

The contactless design fits people who cannot or will not wear a device every day. Common use cases include overnight monitoring of care-facility residents, safety checks for seniors living alone, and loneliness-death prevention programs run by local governments. The same approach also extends to sleep health tracking and early screening for chronic conditions.

## Collaboration Relevance

SIG Corporation suits partners in senior care, digital health, and public welfare. Care facilities, hospitals, insurers, and local governments are natural PoC partners for overnight monitoring and early-warning services. The contactless pad is also a good fit for smart-home, welfare, and remote-patient-monitoring platforms that want vital-sign data without wearables.

For Korea-Europe collaboration, the company's participation in the Germany delegation opens pre-PoC discovery with European care providers, insurers, and health systems. Europe's aging population and strong elderly-care sector make SENIHUG relevant for facility monitoring and home-care pilots.
