## Company Overview

Faddit builds software for fashion production. The company focuses on the workflow from design sketch to technical drawings, patterns, and production requests.

The goal is to reduce manual work in small-batch fashion production. This can help designers, brands, and sewing factories work with clearer files and fewer production errors.

## Technology & Product

[Faddit](https://faddit.co.kr/) is developing an AI fashion production workflow platform. The system can turn vector sketches into CAD pattern outputs and help prepare tech packs, flats, and production documents.

The product direction also includes 2D and 3D review, fabric selection, and production request workflows. These functions are useful when small brands need to move from idea to sample without a large in-house production team.

## Market & Use Cases

Faddit is relevant for independent designers, small fashion brands, pattern makers, and sewing factories. It can support small-batch production, sample development, and digital handoff between design and manufacturing.

The company also connects to the Dongdaemun production ecosystem, where many small factories need clearer digital production data.

## Traction & References

Faddit was selected for TIPS in November 2025. The operating accelerator is CNTTech.

The company is taking preorders and is building around a network of Dongdaemun sewing factories. The team is using ClimateLaunchpad to connect fashion production automation with lower waste and circular fashion goals.

## Collaboration Relevance

Faddit can work with fashion brands, CAD software partners, textile suppliers, sewing factory networks, and sustainability programs. A useful PoC would test whether the software reduces sample-production time and miscommunication between designers and factories.
