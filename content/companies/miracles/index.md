## Company Overview

Miracles develops and sells functional health products under its own brand, [AIBLAB](https://aiblab.kr). The company applies pharmacy knowledge and ingredient delivery technology to raise how well key ingredients work inside the body. Its product lines cover inner beauty, sleep care, and joint care.

## Technology & Product

[AIBLAB](https://www.aiblab.kr/brand/index.html) is the company's consumer brand for functional supplements. The core idea is to improve the body's absorption of active ingredients rather than only increasing the dose.

The lineup is organized into clear categories. Inner beauty products include liposomal glutathione and collagen items. Sleep care includes a plant-based melatonin product (Melazoy). Joint care includes a chondroitin product. The company also offers vitamin D and K2 and cognitive-support items such as phosphatidylserine([AIBLAB](https://aiblab.kr)).

## Market & Use Cases

The main customers are consumers who buy health functional foods for beauty, sleep, joint, and general wellness needs. Products are sold directly through the AIBLAB online store([AIBLAB](https://aiblab.kr)).

Inner beauty and sleep care are both growing parts of the Korean supplement market, and absorption-focused formulations help a brand stand out in a crowded category.

## Traction & References

Miracles was selected for the Ministry of SMEs and Startups TIPS program in November 2025, with Daejeon Center for Creative Economy & Innovation as the operating partner([TIPS](https://www.jointips.or.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=team&wr_id=4015&sfl=wr_26&stx=2025&page=6)). The same operator invested KRW 100 million in the company([Seoul Economic Daily](https://m.sedaily.com/NewsViewAmp/2H0HMV3VNQ)).

Press coverage reports that the AIBLAB brand reached more than KRW 4 billion in sales during the year of the report([Seoul Economic Daily](https://m.sedaily.com/NewsViewAmp/2H0HMV3VNQ)). The company was also selected for the 2026 Gangnam-gu test-bed program.
