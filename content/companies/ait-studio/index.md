## Company Overview

AIT Studio is a digital healthcare company that turns a single camera into a clinical-grade tool for gait analysis. The founding team comes from KAIST mechanical engineering. Its members have backgrounds in automotive computer vision, consulting, and biomechanics. The company holds one of Korea's largest collections of walking data. It builds its products around movement checks that are fast and easy to access.

## Technology & Product

The main product is [MEDISTEP](https://aitstudio.co.kr/en/product/), a walking-analysis device that runs on an iPad. It is markerless, which means nothing is attached to the body: no wearables and no external sensors. The AI runs right on the device and reads how the joints across the whole body move, using just one camera. In about one minute, it produces about 40 walking metrics, such as walking speed, stride length, balance, and joint angles. The product line consists of MEDISTEP M Pro, a Class I medical device for hospitals and clinics, and MEDISTEP M, a non-medical version for fitness, senior care, and wellness settings. It delivers approximately 95% accuracy compared to gold-standard motion-capture systems (Vicon, Qualisys, GAITRite), verified through third-party testing, at a small fraction of their cost.

## Market & Use Cases

MEDISTEP is used by hospitals, rehabilitation clinics, elderly and senior care, and public health screening. The technology works well for quick mobility checks in places where traditional motion labs are too slow or too expensive. That makes it a good fit for both clinical assessments and large-scale community health screening.
