## Company Overview

Heart Planet Company develops smart textile products for wellness and healthcare. Its main product direction is CoreBalance Smart Socks, a wearable system for posture, balance, and gait data.

The company also works on modular product design. A detachable and reusable sensor module can reduce electronic waste when the textile part is replaced.

## Technology & Product

CoreBalance Smart Socks combine textile sensing with a reusable sensor module. The product reads foot-pressure patterns and can support balance, gait, and fall-risk analysis.

The product direction includes smart socks and insole-based applications. Target users include wellness studios, sports users, older adults, and care organizations.

## Market & Use Cases

Relevant markets include pilates and yoga studios, senior wellness programs, rehabilitation support, sports performance, and fall-prevention services.

The product can be used where posture and foot-pressure data need to be collected without large equipment. The detachable sensor approach can also support circular product models.

## Traction & References

Heart Planet Company plans to join CES 2026 Eureka Park through Kwangwoon University support. Early tester data showed about 87 percent accuracy in foot-pressure pattern detection.

The company targets commercialization in the third quarter of 2026. It has also joined youth entrepreneurship and overseas technology commercialization support programs.
