## Company Overview

Smartride is a mobility operations platform company. It builds management software for corporate taxi companies and other commercial passenger transport operators. Many of these operators still run dispatch, attendance, revenue settlement, and vehicle care on paper or in separate tools. Smartride brings this work into one data-based system. The company is based in Seoul and has a research office in Gwangju. It is now using the same operations know-how to support commercial autonomous vehicle services, where vehicles still need dispatch, monitoring, maintenance, and on-site care.

## Technology & Product

The core product is [IMMS](https://www.smartride.co.kr/product/imms), an intelligent mobility management system. It collects driving and operations data from fleet devices, manages driver KPIs, and works on tablets and phones as well as PCs. The company's technology also covers operation management based on digital tachograph (DTG) data and a solution linked to breathalyzer checks. [D.note](https://www.smartride.co.kr/product/dnote) is the driver-side app. Drivers use it to close the day's operation, report fare revenue, and check dispatch schedules remotely. A newer [management tool for taxi company CEOs](https://www.smartride.co.kr/product/taxi) puts dispatch, attendance, payroll, and vehicle data on one dashboard. Managers can ask questions in plain language through an LLM-based assistant, and an AI module flags unusual patterns such as fake trips or abnormal fuel use. Smartride also runs [PICK](https://www.smartride.co.kr/product/pick), a membership-based premium taxi-hailing service. The company develops its own fleet management system (FMS) and uses it for its platform and control services. Its business lines also include the operation, management, and after-care of taxi-type autonomous vehicles.

## Market & Use Cases

The main customers are corporate taxi companies, taxi cooperatives, and bus and other commercial passenger transport operators in Korea. They use the software to cut manual office work, track driver performance, keep drivers longer through welfare and support services, and find revenue losses early. The second market is autonomous mobility services. Autonomous vehicle developers and cities need a partner that can run depots, vehicles, control rooms, and passenger service every day. Smartride covers this operations layer. Its first use cases are commercial passenger transport and mobility services for older people, people with disabilities, and other passengers who need assistance. The company also takes public-sector (B2G) projects and mobility consulting work.

Smartride builds the operating infrastructure and checks service operations([ETNews](https://www.etnews.com/20260616000045)). ## Collaboration Relevance

Smartride fits automakers, autonomous driving developers, and mobility service providers that need an operations partner for commercial fleets in Korea. Useful PoC topics include a service hub for autonomous vehicles that combines dispatch, remote monitoring, maintenance scheduling, and depot work. Another topic is the integration of vehicle data into its fleet management and driver KPI tools. Taxi and bus operators can test the software to digitize daily management. Its Seoul and Gwangju operations and its ties with taxi companies give partners access to real fleets for pilots. For European companies, Smartride can serve as a local operations and data partner when they test commercial vehicle or robotaxi services in Korea.
