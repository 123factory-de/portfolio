## Company Overview

Fabricduct builds fabric duct systems for HVAC airflow control. Its FlowSox system is used in smart farms, logistics centers, factories, cold storage, and other large spaces.

The company focuses on air distribution, not only HVAC capacity. Better airflow can reduce dead zones, temperature gaps, humidity problems, and energy waste.

## Technology & Product

[FlowSox](https://www.fabricduct.kr/) is a fabric duct and design platform. Fabricduct designs, makes, installs, and checks ducts for each site.

Key products and capabilities include:

- FlowSox fabric ducts for cooling, heating, and ventilation.
- Fabric defrost pockets for cold-chain and refrigeration sites.
- Laser-hole airflow design and CFD-based design review.
- Site checks before and after installation.
- Future FlowSox Builder SaaS for HVAC partners and distributors.

## Market & Use Cases

Fabricduct is relevant to energy-intensive facilities. Target users include smart farms, vertical farms, food factories, pharmaceutical factories, logistics centers, cold storage sites, data centers, and HVAC contractors.

Use cases include even air distribution, lower HVAC energy waste, better crop or product quality, faster installation, and safer airflow in large rooms.
