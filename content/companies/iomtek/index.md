## Company Overview

IOMTEK specializes in upcycled materials, developing high-performance composite materials and functional films from waste plastics and industrial by-products. The company manufactures durable structural materials and thermal insulation films designed to lower industrial carbon footprints and support resource circularity.

## Technology & Product

IOMTEK offers two primary technology and product categories:

- **Composite Materials**: Upcycled structural materials developed using industrial by-products and waste plastic. The materials are designed for mechanical durability and cost-efficiency ([IOMTEK Composite Materials](https://iomtek.com/en/composite-materials/)). Applications include aviation, defense, automotive, electronics, and robotics components ([IOMTEK Research Areas](https://iomtek.com/en/research-areas/)).
- **Climate-Responsive Films**: Durable, eco-friendly insulation films fabricated from recycled polymers. These films are engineered for thermal insulation, climate resilience, and energy conservation ([IOMTEK English](https://iomtek.com/en/)).

## Market & Use Cases

IOMTEK serves industries that consume high volumes of structural polymers and insulation materials, such as construction, civil engineering, transport, and manufacturing. Key use cases include recycled composite panels, railway sleepers, industrial packaging, and energy-saving building wraps.

## Traction & References

- IOMTEK was founded in August 2020 as the first corporate spin-off from POSCO's internal startup incubation program ([IOMTEK About](https://iomtek.com/about/)).
- The company secured Seed investment from POSTECH Holdings in 2020 and follow-on funding from Future Science & Technology Holdings in 2021 ([IOMTEK About](https://iomtek.com/about/)).
- IOMTEK was selected for the TIPS startup incubator program in April 2021 ([IOMTEK About](https://iomtek.com/about/)).
- The company was selected by the Korea Railroad Research Institute (KRRI) in June 2021 to develop composite railway sleepers ([IOMTEK About](https://iomtek.com/about/)).
