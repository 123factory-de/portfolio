## Company Overview

HeatAI develops automated Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) software for building energy retrofits. The platform monitors heat-pump operational data in real time to calculate seasonal performance factor (SPF), verify energy savings, and generate audit-ready reports essential for public subsidy compliance and carbon credit markets.

## Technology & Product

The [HeatAI platform](https://www.heatai.co.kr/) works with heat-pump operating data and converts it into MRV outputs. It checks data quality, tracks seasonal performance factor metrics, manages uncertainty, and creates evidence bundles.

The platform also keeps change logs and data integrity records. These functions are important when energy-saving data must be reviewed by public institutions, auditors, or carbon-market stakeholders.

## Market & Use Cases

HeatAI is relevant for public buildings, energy agencies, building owners, and heat-pump project operators. It can support projects where energy-saving performance must be measured after installation.

Useful PoC settings include public-sector retrofit programs, district-level heat-pump projects, and energy-efficiency projects that need reliable operating data.
