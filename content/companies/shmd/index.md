## Company Overview

SHMD develops AI-based brain health solutions for stroke prevention and early detection. The company pairs a wearable cerebral blood-flow device with a self-screening app, so users can check brain health risks without a hospital visit. It also runs an AI Medtech Lab in Seoul.

## Technology & Product

SHMD focuses on non-invasive brain health hardware and software:

- **CEREBAND**: A wearable AI device for stroke prevention, built on transcranial Doppler (TCD) sensing to measure and monitor cerebral blood flow.
- **BRAINCHECK.AI**: A self-screening app that combines four assessments in one place — stroke risk, cognitive function, carotid sound analysis, and headache evaluation. The carotid screening uses a patented AI algorithm, and the app was co-developed with the Korean Stroke Society.

## Market & Use Cases

The main users are people who want to track stroke and brain health risk at home, without clinical equipment. Because the screening runs on a smartphone in a few minutes, it fits preventive health programs, employer wellness checks, and follow-up monitoring for people with high blood pressure or other risk factors. CEREBAND extends this into continuous wearable monitoring of cerebral blood flow.
