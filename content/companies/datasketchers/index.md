## Company Overview

Data Sketchers develops AI-driven compliance and ESG management software for global supply chains. The platform automates complex document verification, aligning supplier safety and sustainability data with global buyer standards and food regulatory frameworks, significantly reducing administrative risks and audit times.

## Technology & Product

[Datasketchers](https://data-sketchers.com/en/) uses RAG-based AI to match supplier audit data with regulations, ESG frameworks, and buyer standards. The system finds compliance gaps and turns them into practical reports.

The company also operates D-SKET services such as web builders, canvas tools, and event support. These services give the team delivery experience with business users and digital workflows.

## Market & Use Cases

The main use case is supplier ESG and compliance work in food and FMCG supply chains. Enterprise teams can use the tool to review supplier documents, identify risk, and prepare audit responses faster.

It is also relevant for SMEs that need to meet buyer requirements but do not have a large ESG or legal team.

## Traction & References

Datasketchers has venture certification, a company research institute, software business registration, and public procurement vendor status. The company has joined several government support programs and has more than 15 customers.

The company has also received financing support through a direct loan and guarantee program. SK AX and Paradise Group vendor certification are part of its business proof points.
