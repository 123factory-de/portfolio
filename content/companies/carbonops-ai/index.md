## Company Overview

CarbonOps AI is a carbon and ESG workflow project from Trobai. It focuses on small and mid-sized suppliers in the shipbuilding value chain.

The product helps suppliers understand carbon requirements from shipyards, class societies, and buyers. It then turns production and material data into practical reduction plans.

## Technology & Product

[Trobai](https://www.trobai.com/) develops neuro-symbolic AI technology. CarbonOps AI applies this approach to carbon accounting and supplier ESG work.

The system reads technical requirements, ESG checklists, energy use, and material data. It maps emission factors and creates reduction plans under cost, delivery, quality, and certification constraints.

## Market & Use Cases

The first market is Korean shipbuilding equipment suppliers. Relevant users include companies that make valves, pumps, piping, metal parts, electrical parts, and coating-related components.

Large shipyards and global customers are asking suppliers for better carbon data. CarbonOps AI can help smaller suppliers respond with structured data and clear action plans.

## Traction & References

Trobai has three patent applications related to its neuro-symbolic AI work. The team has also received recognition in tourism open innovation and AI startup programs.

CarbonOps AI is at sketch, mockup, and rendering stage. The team is using ClimateLaunchpad to shape the climate-tech use case and test the first customer workflow.

## Collaboration Relevance

CarbonOps AI can work with shipyards, equipment suppliers, ESG consulting teams, and manufacturing groups that manage supplier data. A useful PoC would test carbon data collection with a small group of suppliers and compare the output with buyer reporting needs.

The topic is also relevant for Korea-Europe trade. European buyers and regulations are increasing pressure on supply-chain carbon reporting, and Korean shipbuilding suppliers need practical tools to respond.
