## Company Overview

FUST Lab develops DEBREX, a focused ultrasonic dispersion and emulsification platform for chemical and battery industry applications. The equipment is designed for high-performance nanoscale dispersion, including materials that are difficult to disperse through conventional methods.

DEBREX focuses on uniform nano-dispersion, precise power and frequency control, and continuous in-line processing for formulation development and production.

## Technology & Product

DEBREX applies cylindrical ultrasonic processing to concentrate energy at the center of a controlled flow path. An integrated cooling system helps stabilize temperature, reduce overheating risk, and support continuous operation.

The platform supports:

- High-precision and high-performance dispersion.
- Dispersant-free nano-dispersion where formulations allow it.
- Uniform dispersion of carbon-based nanomaterials and emerging advanced materials.
- SWCNT and MXene dispersion for secondary battery applications.
- Nickel and metal powder dispersion for advanced electronic materials.
- Lab, pilot, and production-scale equipment configurations.

FUST Lab also provides automated cleaning, real-time frequency tracking, flow-rate control, and display-based monitoring for operation and maintenance.

## Strategic Highlights

- DEBREX supports lab-scale, pilot-scale, and production-scale operation.
- Target materials include SWCNT, MXene, carbon-based nanomaterials, metal powders, and other advanced materials.
- The technology emphasizes reproducibility through real-time resonant frequency detection and power/frequency control.
- The platform is positioned for chemical and battery applications where dispersion quality directly affects final product performance.
