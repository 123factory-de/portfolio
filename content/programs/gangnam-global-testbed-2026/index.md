## Program Overview

Companies participating in the **2026 Gangnam Global Testbed Program**, where 123factory serves as an operating partner, are gathered here.

The program includes global company matching, 1:1 meeting support, capability-building support, Pre-PoC support, and follow-on linkage support. Its focus areas include mobility, retail tech, smart health and medical tech, chemicals and materials, optical technology, sustainability and climate tech, industrial technology and manufacturing innovation, AI and software, and food tech and consumer goods.
