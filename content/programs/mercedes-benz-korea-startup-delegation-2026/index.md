## Program Overview
As part of the **Global Substantiation Challenge**, Korean startups are visiting Stuttgart to explore business opportunities and build connections within the German innovation ecosystem.

The program brings together innovative Korean startups and stakeholders from Germany’s **automotive, technology, business, and innovation ecosystem**, creating opportunities to exchange ideas and explore potential collaborations.

The participating startups are looking to connect with **German companies, research institutions, investors, industry experts, and potential business partners**. During their visit to Stuttgart, they will have opportunities to introduce their technologies, exchange perspectives, and explore potential **PoCs, technology partnerships, and market entry opportunities** in Germany and Europe.

## Networking Event
As part of the program, **123 Factory, Y&Archer, and Mercedes-Benz Korea** are collaborating to create a networking opportunity connecting the participating Korean startups with relevant stakeholders from the **Stuttgart and broader German innovation ecosystem**.

The event is designed to facilitate informal introductions, technology exchange, and discussions around potential business and technology collaboration.

## Participating Startups
The participating Korean startups bring technologies and solutions across **automotive software, AI, mobility, EV battery technology, autonomous driving, automotive electronics, and robotics**.

Each company profile introduces its **technology, products or services, current market focus, and potential areas for collaboration**, including **PoCs, joint projects, technology partnerships, distribution, and market entry**.
