## Program Overview

In September 2026, eight Korean deep-tech startups traveled to Düsseldorf, Germany, through the overseas expansion program of the **Deep-Tech Youth Startup Academy**. The program is run by the **Korea SMEs and Startups Agency (KOSME)** with **Openknowl**, and **123 Factory** as the local partner in Germany.

Over four days (September 9–12, 2026), the companies focused on concrete market-entry outcomes rather than a study tour: pre-PoC discovery, industry partner and customer matching, and investor networking across Germany and Europe.

The on-site program included a visit to the Düsseldorf City Korea Desk, a German market-entry seminar, a booth and IR at **Future Tech Fest 2026**, company pitches at **ERGO & ERGO ScaleHub**'s International Startup Booster, and a visit to **Henkel**.

## Participating Companies

The eight startups span AI and enterprise data, synthetic data infrastructure, deep-tech materials, digital health, UAV safety, and publishing technology.
